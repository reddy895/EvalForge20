# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in EvalForge20 a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give actionable feedback.
- Focus on the technical issue, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish others' private information.
- Do not submit fabricated benchmarks or misleading claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
